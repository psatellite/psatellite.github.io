SafeGuidanceSim:

Path: FormationFlying/SafeGuidance

% Relative dynamic simulation for two LEO satellites with safe guidance.
 Includes differential disturbance forces for J2, drag and solar.
 Explicitly calls SafeGuidance maneuver planning algorithm.
 
 Assumptions:

 - satellites have different masses 
 - satellites have differential area for solar pressure
 - satellites have differential area for drag
 - disturbances do not measurably affect reference orbit over simulation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Form:
  simData = FFRelSim( simOptions )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Sim options fields:
    elRef  [a,i,W,w,e,M]
    nOrbits
    nPts     Discretization points per orbit
    distOpt  Flags for [solar drag J2]
    harmonicOrder
    control
    params
    noise
    flags
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
