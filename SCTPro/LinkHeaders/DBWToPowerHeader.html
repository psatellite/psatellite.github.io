% Convert from dBW to power.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   power = DBWToPower( dBW )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   dBW           (1,:)    dBW

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   power         (1,:)    Power (W)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
