DBPower:

Path: Link/LinkUtilities

% Power conversion to decibels. dB = 20 log10(s).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   dB = DBPower( p )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   p       (:)     Power
                        
   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   dB      (:)     Decibels
                         
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
	References:	Lambert, S. G. and W. L. Casey (1995.) Laser
               Communications in Space. Artech, Boston.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
