QESilicon:

Path: Imaging/Utilities

% Quantum efficiency of silicon
 Uses the values for photovoltaic efficiency.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   [qE,r] = QESilicon( lambda )
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   lambda	(1,:) Wavelength (nm)

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   qE      (1,:) Quantum efficiency
   r       (1,:) Responsivity

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
