% Starfire Mars Mission Crewed CAD Model
 You can change parameters to resize the vehicle. This designs a fusion
 propelled spacecraft to take an Orion capsule to Mars. Sizes the mission
 and builds a model of the propulsion stage.

 Loads MarsLanderAeroshell.mat

 If exporting is requested, this creates StarfireTripSegments.tex,
 StarfireParams.tex, StarfireMarsMass.tex, and StarfireMarsPropulsion.mat
