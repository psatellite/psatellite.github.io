StellarIrradiance:

Path: SC/Sensor

% Computes the stellar irradiance.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
    h = StellarIrradiance( m, starType, detectorType )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   m            (1,n)     Bolometric magnitude
   starType     {n}       Star type
   detectorType {n}       Detector type

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   h            (1,n)     Irradiance (W/cm^2)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
