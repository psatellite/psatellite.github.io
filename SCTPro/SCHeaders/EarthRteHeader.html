EarthRte:

Path: SC/Ephem

% Computes the mean earth rate.
   See also MSidDay.

   Since version 1.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   w = EarthRte( jD )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   jD              (1,1)   Julian date (day)

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   w               (1,1)    Mean earth rate (rad/sec)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
	References:	The Astronomical Almanac for the Year 1993, U.S. Government
               Printing Office, 1993, p. B6. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
