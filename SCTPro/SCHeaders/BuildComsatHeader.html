BuildComsat:

Path: SC/CAD

% Build up a model of a geosynchronous communications satellite.
 This script demonstrates many of the methods of incorporating spacecraft
 components into a CAD model. After the model is finished, it is displayed with
 DrawSCPlugIn. The thrusters are cycled just to show you where they are. 

 To see
 there the spacecraft is relative to the Earth, zoom out repeatedly once the
 loop is finished. You can then scale the spacecraft up to see it better. Turn
 on ambient light to brighten the spacecraft surfaces.
