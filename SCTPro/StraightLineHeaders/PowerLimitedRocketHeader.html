% Power-Limited Rocket (SLPL) Demo
 This set of functions will find the optimal mission for a straight-line
 trajectory with a fixed power level, allowing acceleration to vary.
 (Acceleration becomes infinite at the switch point.) 

 See also: SLPLDataStructure, SLPLSolver, Plot2D
