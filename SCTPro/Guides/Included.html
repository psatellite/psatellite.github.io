What's Included

The Spacecraft Design Toolbox is a library of MATLAB functions organized into modules by subject — covering spacecraft dynamics, mission design, estimation, orbit mechanics, propulsion, and more.

Fundamental Modules
AerospaceUtils
Functions shared by the Aircraft and Spacecraft toolboxes — atmosphere models, CAD tools, and coordinate transformations.
CubeSat
Small-scale satellite design, analysis, and simulation tools.
Link
RF and optical link analysis tools.
Missions
In-depth design examples including the hypothetical geosynchronous satellite ComStar.
Orbit
Orbit mechanics, maneuver planning, fuel budgets, and high-fidelity simulation.
Propulsion
Electric and chemical propulsion analysis and sizing.
SC
Attitude dynamics, pointing budgets, basic orbit dynamics, environment models, CAD models, ephemeris, sensor and actuator modeling.
SCPro
High-fidelity models for environment, sensors, and actuators. Attitude profiles with constraints. Landers.
SpacecraftEstimation
Attitude and orbit estimation. Stellar attitude determination and Kalman filtering.
Thermal
Thermal modeling including isothermal and thermal equilibrium analysis.
Support Modules
Common
Control, time conversions, graphics, quaternions, and general utilities.
Electrical
Power system component modeling.
Imaging
Image processing tools for spacecraft and optical systems.
Math
Analysis, solvers, integration, and mathematical utilities.
Plotting
Plotting tools for complex simulations including animation support.
Power
Solar cell efficiency tools and wire gauge diameter and resistance functions.
Add-On Modules
Interplanetary Pack
Interplanetary transfer and missions including cislunar dynamics, Lambert solvers, and trajectory optimization.
Formation Flying
Formation flying guidance, control, collision avoidance, and decentralized satellite frameworks.
Launch Vehicle
Launch vehicle design, multi-stage analysis, gravity turn, reentry, and TSTO simulations.
Optical Navigation
Navigation from planets and stars, terrain-relative navigation, and neural network guidance.
SAAD
Spin axis attitude determination using batch methods, Kalman filtering, and GUI tools.
Solar Sail
Solar sail control, mission analysis, disturbance modeling, and trajectory optimization.
Documentation: The core modules — SC, SpacecraftEstimation, Orbit, Link, Propulsion, and Thermal — are described in the main PDF user's guide, included with the Professional Edition. The Formation Flying, Launch Vehicle, Solar Sail, and SAAD add-on modules each have their own PDF user's guide and can be purchased separately.
